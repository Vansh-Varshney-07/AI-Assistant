## Question

## Proposed direction — not a resolved decision

No known critical memory/authorization failure; publish actual source/receipt/failure evidence and blocked/unsupported capabilities. Treat suggested numeric thresholds as proposals and finite test passes as evidence, not perfect reliability.

## Comments
